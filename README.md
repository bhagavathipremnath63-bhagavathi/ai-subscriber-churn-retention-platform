📊 Subscriber Attrition & Revenue Recovery Analysis
🔍 Business Problem

A subscription-based business is facing high churn (54.35%), leading to significant revenue loss (~$3M). There is limited visibility into which customer behaviors and segments are driving attrition.

🎯 Objective
Identify key drivers of churn
Analyze impact of subscription types and customer behavior
Quantify revenue leakage
Provide data-driven recommendations

📁 Dataset
10,000+ subscriber records
Includes contract type, billing cycle, support interactions, and churn status

🛠 Tools Used
Power BI (DAX, Power Query)
Excel

📈 Key Insights
Monthly subscription users show highest churn risk
Day-21 payment delay acts as a churn trigger point
Customers with 6+ support interactions show severe retention drop
Total revenue loss estimated at ~$3M

💡 Recommendations
Promote quarterly and annual subscription plans
Implement proactive payment reminders before Day-21
Improve customer support experience to reduce repeat issues

🧠 Business Impact
Performed requirement analysis, KPI definition, and churn driver identification to support data-driven decision making.
